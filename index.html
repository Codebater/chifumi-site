(01) The work

Across Asia, Europeand Africa, I create visual statements that transform placesinto stories.

What defines my work? A universal symbol: the hand.

Whether inspired by the gestures of Apsaras in Cambodia or sacred mudras from India, hands become the language through which I express identity, emotions and movement.

(02) The hand as a language

Chi · Fou · Mi

In French, chi-fou-mi is the playground chant before rock, paper, scissors: a game played with hands. A fitting name for an artist whose whole visual language is the hand.

01/03

01 — Cambodia

Apsara

The celestial dancers of Angkor speak with their fingers. Their gestures run through the walls painted in Phnom Penh and Siem Reap.

02 — India

Mudra

Sacred hand positions from Hindu and Buddhist iconography, painted large on buildings from Coimbatore to Chennai and Varanasi.

03 — The street

Hand signs

Mythological stories, ethnic patterns and hand-sign communication: one visual language, read the same way in every city.

(05) Beyond walls

Only murals?Not quite!

When not painting walls, he crafts hand-pulled prints in limited editions and experiments with digital layers that bring his work to life through mobile interaction. From traditional techniques to immersive formats, his art extends across mediums, but always carries the same signature: the hand as a symbol.

01

Screen printing

His prints are hand-pulled, produced in limited editions, and share the visual language of his murals: expressive hands, symbolic gestures and bold forms rooted in cultural aesthetics.

Each silkscreen is made with traditional techniques: layered ink, tactile paper, numbered and signed. A fragment of his street work in a collectible, gallery-ready format.

02

Augmented reality

By blending physical murals with digital motion, he creates hybrid artworks that come alive through your smartphone.

Hands that move, gestures that evolve, stories that reveal themselves: AR becomes a space where street art meets animation, and the viewer becomes part of the experience.
