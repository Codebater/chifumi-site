(About)

Born in France · Based in Cambodia

I'mChifumi

A French muralist painting stories on walls around the world.

Internationally renowned for majestic, intricate large-scale murals scattered across the globe, Chifumi blends mythological stories, ethnic patterns and hand-sign communication into one unique style.

His passion for using his creative voice to raise awareness of societal and environmental issues has built him a reputation as an engaged artist, driven by a desire to make an impact.

Born in France, Chifumi moved to Cambodia after graduating from art school, a move that sparked a lifelong fascination with ancient Asian heritage and mythology.

His passion for art in public spaces led him to the Cambodia Urban Art Festival: since 2015 he has organised, every year, the first and major event around street creativity, pushing young local artists to express themselves.
