Collabs

Trusted by brands, institutions and places that matter.

Limited-edition cans, a rum bottled in a hundred copies, a hand-drawn hotel map, a painted car: the same hand and the same stories, at every scale.

01Coca-Cola2023

A celebration of Cambodian tradition through art and fizz

Khmer New Year limited edition

Cambodia's Khmer New Year celebrations got a refreshing twist in 2023 with an artistic partnership with Coca-Cola.

Together we created three limited-edition cans. Each one is more than a vessel for a drink: it is a canvas celebrating the rich tapestry of Cambodian culture and traditions.

02Samai DistilleryLimited edition

Samai × Chifumi

100 bottles, Kampot pepper rum

Based in Phnom Penh, Samai is Cambodia's first rum distillery. Since 2014 they have made hand-crafted premium rums from locally grown ingredients, in a process that pays tribute to age-old rum-making traditions.

We teamed up to produce a limited edition of 100 bottles of Kampot pepper flavoured rum.

03Rosewood Phnom PenhHand-drawn map

A hand-drawn map of the hidden Phnom Penh

Beyond the tourist trail

Forget boring guides: the Rosewood Phnom Penh now has a bespoke map bursting with personality. Hand-drawn alleys flow like rivers, Apsaras guide you to secrets, and markets burst with colour.

More than a map, an adventure into the soul of the city. Unfold it, trace your own path, and discover a city with layers of vibrant life.

04Hyundai2018

Ancient Khmer swirls meet neon cityscapes

Hand-painted Veloster

A one-off hand-painted Hyundai Veloster. Gleaming curves carry intricate patterns that whisper tales of temples and urban rhythms.

Tradition reimagined, one stroke at a time.
