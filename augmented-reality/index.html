With Julien Croyal · Artivive

Walls that move

By blending physical murals with digital motion, he creates hybrid artworks that come alive through your smartphone.

Hands that move, gestures that evolve, stories that reveal themselves: AR becomes a space where street art meets animation, and the viewer becomes part of the experience.

01

Paint the wall

The mural is designed and painted by hand, like every other Chifumi wall. It stands on its own, with or without a phone.

02

Animate the story

Motion designer Julien Croyal brings the artwork to life: hands that move, gestures that evolve, legends that unfold.

03

Point your phone

Open the Artivive app, aim it at the wall and the painting starts to move. The viewer becomes part of the work.

01 — Schiltigheim, France — 2023

A vol d'oiseau

Schilick: la beauté des différences — “Le beau dans la ville”

Plays from YouTube

Winner of the Le Beau Dans La Ville contest, a mural project initiated by Le Foyer Moderne and the municipality of Schiltigheim.

Painted with augmented reality in collaboration with motion designer Julien Croyal, “A vol d’oiseau” highlights the beauty of the local culture and its iconic wildlife.

02 — Phnom Penh, Cambodia — Aquatic centre

The Churning of the Ocean of Milk

Northbridge International School, Phnom Penh

Plays from YouTube

Commissioned by Northbridge International School of Phnom Penh for their new aquatic centre. Once again with Julien Croyal, a French motion designer based in Paris: concept artwork and mural by Chifumi, animation by Julien, brought to life with the Artivive app.

The myth of the churning of the ocean of milk is one of the most iconic legends of Cambodia. Its most famous rendition runs across the bas-reliefs of a dedicated gallery at Angkor Wat, and the causeways of Angkor Thom show it in sculptural form.
